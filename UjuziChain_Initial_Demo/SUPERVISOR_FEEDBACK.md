# Supervisor feedback update

Changes made for this phase:

- All registered candidates can browse the full job list.
- Match scores are displayed as guidance only.
- A candidate can apply even when the match score is low.
- A role-specific simulation can be completed and the score attached to the application.
- The candidate portal now has separate Jobs, Applications and Simulations screens.
- The Overview screen shows the planned job-ingestion layer without presenting the external feeds as already implemented.
- External jobs are planned through approved APIs, partner feeds, ATS connectors and permitted integrations.
- The frontend styling was reduced to a simple working-dashboard layout for the initial demo.
