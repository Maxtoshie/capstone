Trust Architecture

The On-Chain Verification Process

Separating who issues, who holds, and who verifies credentials according to the W3C decentralized trust triad.

1

The Issuer (ALU)

African Leadership University registers as an authorized issuer. It computes the hash of the graduate's diploma and calls issueCredential(bytes32 id) on Sepolia.

2

The Holder (Candidate)

The student controls their Ethereum address. They hold their credential ID (UC-2026-001) and present it with their verified wallet without revealing sensitive PII.

3

The Verifier (Recruiter)

Employers run a gasless verifyCredential query against our smart contract. The EVM checks the state and mathematically confirms legitimacy in milliseconds.
