Cryptographic Verification Pipeline

How UjuziChain replaces manual resume checks with zero-knowledge, deterministic validation.

1

Client-Side Document Digest

Degrees and certificates are processed in the applicant's browser using native Web Crypto APIs. Only the irreversible SHA-256 hash is produced—no raw PDF files are ever stored on a central server.

2

On-Chain Registry Attestation

Authorized academic institutions commit credential digests to the UjuziChain Ethereum Sepolia smart contract with immutable timestamps and revocation capabilities.

3

Deterministic 70% Match Gate

Our client-side competency algorithm measures candidate skill vectors against required job criteria. Only scores ≥70% unlock instantaneous One-Click Auto Apply.

4

Applied Competency Simulation

Candidates scoring under 70% can unlock application access by completing an interactive engineering simulation to prove applied knowledge regardless of traditional background.

UjuziChain Quick Setup Guide

1. Install MetaMask

Ensure you have MetaMask or an EIP-1193 compatible wallet extension installed in your browser.

2. Connect to Sepolia Testnet

UjuziChain runs on Ethereum Sepolia Testnet. Switch your wallet network to Sepolia. You can obtain free SepoliaETH from any public faucet.

3. Login to the Portal

Click "Launch Platform" or "Applicant Portal", connect your wallet, and approve the one-time cryptographic challenge to authenticate.

4. Try the Verification Engine

Upload a demo credential PDF in the Dashboard to generate its SHA-256 digest, or verify existing credentials directly against the smart contract.

Terms of Service

1. Platform Purpose

UjuziChain is a decentralized academic credential and competency verification platform. All blockchain transactions occur on the Ethereum Sepolia Testnet for research and academic demonstration purposes.

2. Self-Sovereign Identity

Users are solely responsible for safeguarding their private keys and wallet credentials. UjuziChain does not hold custody of user keys or funds.

3. Document Privacy

Document digests (SHA-256 hashes) are generated locally in the browser. UjuziChain does not store or process raw document files or personally identifiable information on centralized servers.

Privacy Policy

Zero-PII Storage Architecture

UjuziChain adheres to strict zero-PII storage principles. Uploaded certificates and transcripts are processed exclusively in client-side memory via the Web Cryptography API. Only mathematical cryptographic digests are anchored to the public blockchain registry.

On-Chain Transparency

Interactions with Ethereum Sepolia smart contracts are public and immutable. By attesting or verifying credentials, users acknowledge that the associated cryptographic hash and wallet addresses are recorded on the public ledger.
