About UjuziChain

Decentralized Trust for the Future of African Tech

Traditional hiring is bogged down by unverified resumes and manual credential checks. UjuziChain removes intermediaries by combining on-chain smart contract verification with real-world skill simulations.

On-Chain Registry

Credentials issued by certified institutions (like ALU) are immutably signed to our Sepolia smart contract, making academic fraud mathematically impossible.

Simulated Job Match

Candidates prove readiness through role-specific scenario tasks, giving hiring teams verified proof of engineering ability before scheduling an interview.

Zero Gatekeeping

Non-custodial MetaMask authentication lets talent from Nairobi, Kigali, and across Africa maintain sovereign ownership of their career identity.
