Why UjuziChain?

Hiring across borders still means emailing a registrar and waiting weeks. A PDF can be edited in minutes, so many employers skip the check and hope for the best.

Hiring platforms also keep CVs and ID documents in one database. One breach exposes everyone. And keyword filters throw out bootcamp graduates before anyone looks at what they can do.

UjuziChain changes three things. Approved institutions record credentials on a public ledger. Documents are fingerprinted in your browser and never uploaded. Applicants who match most of a role's skills apply directly, and everyone else can prove themselves in a short simulation.

How it works

01

An institution records it

An approved university signs one transaction with its wallet. The record is public and cannot be quietly changed.

02

A recruiter checks it

Paste the credential ID. The app reads the registry for free and shows a receipt with the block it was read at.

03

Skills decide the rest

Your skills are compared with the role. At 70% or more you can apply. Below that, a short simulation opens the door.
