Terms of Service

Welcome to UjuziChain. By connecting an Ethereum wallet address, you agree to these Terms of Service governing decentralized talent matching across East Africa.

1. Non-Custodial Sovereign Identity

Authentication operates entirely via public key cryptography (EIP-1193 / MetaMask). UjuziChain does not hold custody of your private keys or personal credentials.

2. On-Chain Verification & Integrity

Academic credentials recorded on the Ethereum Sepolia smart contract (0xA40d77d6e31F5044C7151dAd78C8bA6C3313132F) are public, immutable, and non-repudiable.

3. Role Assessments & Gating

4. Governing Jurisdiction

Decentralized Privacy Policy

UjuziChain is architected to eliminate centralized data honeypots in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019) and GDPR principles.

1. Zero Plaintext Document Storage

Documents uploaded to UjuziChain are hashed on your client machine using the browser's native Web Crypto API (SHA-256). Raw PDF bytes and personal contact information are never stored on central servers.

2. Non-Custodial Identification

We do not store passwords, session cookies, or tracking pixels. Your identifier is your public Ethereum address ($secp256k1$).

3. Blockchain Transparency

Smart contract assertions only record unique credential hashes (bytes32) and university degree titles. No personally identifiable student records (PII) are published un-hashed to the ledger.

4. Data Sovereignty

Candidates retain complete ownership of their career identity. Closing or clearing your browser session eliminates local persistence without leaving residual database records.
