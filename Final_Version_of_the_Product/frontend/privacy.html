Privacy Policy

In short. UjuziChain is a university capstone prototype. Please do not enter real personal data. Your documents never leave your device. Anything written to the blockchain is public and cannot be deleted. Skill scoring is automated, you can see why you got your score, and you can ask for a human review.

1. Who is responsible

UjuziChain is built by Maxwel Okoth as a capstone project at African Leadership University (ALU). Contact for privacy questions: [author's ALU email].

3. Why we use it

Only to let institutions record credentials, let recruiters check them, and score skills against jobs. You agree to this when you tick the consent box before signing in. You can stop at any time by not using the app and deleting your local data.

3a. Fees

Applicants and institutions pay no platform fee (institutions pay normal network gas to write records). Reading and verifying a credential is free for everyone. A recruiter may choose to pay a small fee to record a certified check on-chain. The fee is capped by the smart contract, is shown before you sign, and is sent to the platform owner to cover running costs. You can never be charged more than the cap.

4. What is public and permanent

A blockchain cannot delete records. Institutions must use a pseudonymous student reference, never a name, national ID or email. The app builds the student reference from a short hash of your wallet address and builds the credential ID from your wallet address and document fingerprint, so neither shows your name or the fingerprint itself. Because the record is permanent, we cannot erase it. We can only mark a credential as revoked.

5. Automated skill scoring

The app compares your skills with a job's required skills and shows a score from 30 to 95. Institution, name and background are not used. A score of 70 or more lets you apply directly. A lower score asks you to take a short practical simulation. The job page lists which required skills matched and which are missing. If a result looks unfair, choose Request human review on that job and a person will look at your profile.

6. Your rights

You can see, correct and delete the data we hold about you, object to processing, and ask for human review of automated decisions. Data in your browser can be removed instantly with the button at the end of this page. Data on the blockchain cannot be erased, as explained above. To use a right or complain, write to the contact above. In Kenya you may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, and in Rwanda to the National Cyber Security Authority.

7. Security

We never hold your private key. Documents stay on your device. Pages are served over HTTPS. This is a prototype and has not had a professional security audit.

8. Children and changes

The app is for adults aged 18 and over. If this policy changes, the version and date above will change.
